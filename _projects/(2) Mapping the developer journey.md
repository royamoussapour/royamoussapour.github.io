#### Goal
Capital One needed to create a comprehensive mapping of the current-state developer experience from pull request to deployment to identify manual bottlenecks preventing continuous deployment. 

#### Process
 I led a multi-phase discovery effort that included contextual inquiry sessions, in-depth interviews, and stakeholder workshops with developers across lines of business.

<!-- ![preview](https://i.imgur.com/NfEgqxg.png)
-->

#### Impact
The journey map we created was adopted by leadership as a strategic artifact for 2026 roadmap planning. 

#### Key Insights
<ul>
  <li>Developers often default to manual testing due to low confidence in the reliability of automated test suites and test coverage</li>
  <li>Test suite and pipeline performance are critical to continuous deployment, and developers worry about the impact of additional testing on slow workflows</li>
  <li>Developers use QA environments as staging environments to build confidence of downstream impacts of their code</li>
</ul>

#### Deliverables
Deliverables: A research report and end-to-end journey map highlighting areas of opportunity for investment and automation to move towards continuous deployment.
