# How I Work Draft

### Strategic partnership

Research is only as good as its **integration and impact on product development.**

- **Embedded:** I’ve worked as the sole researcher within a product and design team, influencing long-term roadmaps through generative and evaluative research.
- **Consulting:**I’ve provided specialized research across diverse areas for teams needing targeted research.

### Structured methodology

I engage in research with a focus on **rigor, clarity, and actionability of results.**

- **Research plans:** I begin projects with structured research plans whenever time permits to align stakeholders on research questions, hypotheses, and goals.
- **Frameworks:** I provide consistent and actionable deliverables, partnering with product and design on turning research insights into designs, strategies, and outcomes.

### Democratization and mentorship

I love **empowering designers and product managers** to conduct their own research.

- **Coaching:** I mentor designers and product managers to engage in tactical research safely and effectively.
- **Standardization:** I create and share templates and guides to ensure democratized work maintains high-quality standards.

###Research operations and ethics

Good research requires a **foundation of trust.** I approach this proactively.

- **Ethics and consent:** I prioritize participant trust and data integrity by maintaining consent workflows and ethical data practices.
- **Inclusion:** My training has focused on equity-centered design, and I consider the inclusion of diverse participants in research as critical to creating equitable and accessible products.
