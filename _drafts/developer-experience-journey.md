# Identifying manual developer processes from pull request to deployment

## Goal
Capital One needed to create a comprehensive mapping of the current-state developer experience from pull request to deployment to identify manual bottlenecks preventing continuous deployment.

## Process
I led a multi-phase discovery effort that included contextual inquiry sessions, in-depth interviews, and stakeholder workshops with developers across lines of business.

## Impact
The journey map we created was adopted by leadership as a strategic artifact for 2026 roadmap planning.

## Key insights
- Developers often default to manual testing due to low confidence in the reliability of automated test suites and test coverage.
- Test suite and pipeline performance are critical to continuous deployment, and developers worry about the impact of additional testing on slow workflows.
- Developers use QA environments as staging environments to build confidence in downstream impacts of their code.

## Deliverables
A research report and end-to-end journey map highlighting areas of opportunity for investment and automation to move towards continuous deployment.
